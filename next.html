The Phenix toolkit

A little more detail. A lot more possibility.

Each feature is designed to help you get unstuck, communicate clearly, and make progress on the work that matters to you.

Conversation that keeps up

Ask a question in your own words, add context, and follow up naturally. Phenix can help explain an unfamiliar topic, summarize a long idea, brainstorm options, or rework a draft to fit your audience.

Step-by-step problem-solving

Break a complicated challenge into smaller pieces. Explore possible approaches, compare trade-offs, outline an action plan, and refine the result as you learn more along the way.

Expressive voice options

Shape spoken experiences with different voice styles and adjustable character. Voice changing can help a narration feel warmer, more energetic, or better suited to a creative project.

Images from imagination

Describe a subject, setting, mood, and visual direction to develop an original image concept. Iterate on your prompt to explore different compositions, styles, or details.

Presentation-ready thinking

Start with a topic or a rough brief and shape it into a clear flow: an opening, key points, supporting examples, and a conclusion. Build a slide outline you can polish and present.

One connected workflow

Move from a conversation to a creative brief, then to imagery or a presentation outline. Keep the momentum going without starting from scratch every time an idea changes.
